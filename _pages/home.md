## About
I'm CEO and Co-Founder of **[Neural Nurture](https://www.ntwo.ai)**, an applied AI lab that turns NLP and computer-vision research into products people use. Our first product, **Tiaris**, is an AI flight-search assistant live in the US and India: travelers describe a trip in plain English, and Tiaris searches, ranks and explains the best options. We're also building privacy-first AI tools, with 250+ people already on the waitlist. I co-founded the company with Dr. Dinesh Daultani, who leads computer vision and previously worked at Woven by Toyota and Rakuten.

## Background
I've spent 15+ years shipping machine learning at scale, across research labs and some of the largest consumer platforms in Asia.

- **Rakuten, Group Manager, NLP (2021–2023):** Led a 15-scientist NLP group across E-Commerce, Finance and Travel. Delivered a keyword ad-bidding optimizer for external search engines and a knowledge-graph-based product attribute extractor, and contributed to Rakuten AI 7B, the company's in-house Japanese LLM.
- **Amazon Japan, Sr. Technical Program Manager, Applied ML (2019–2021):** Ran ML programs across 13 countries. Co-invented a sequence-to-sequence ad-generation system for paid search, and shipped a neural Japanese tokenizer into six business use cases.
- **Rakuten, Head of NLP and Deep Learning Researcher (2017–2019):** Built a deep-learning fraud detection platform for Rakuten Card, user-retargeting models for the travel business, and an unsupervised summarization system for investment analysts. Helped establish the Rakuten India research lab and co-founded the AI Trainers program.
- **NEC Central Research Laboratories, Researcher (2014–2017):** Developed deep-learning algorithms for NEC's SX-ACE supercomputer that cut CNN inference time by up to 95%. Visiting Scholar at UC Berkeley's [Swarm Lab](https://www.theswarmlab.com/) (2015), leading an IoT project on vertical mobility.
- **Accenture, Associate Software Engineer (2010–2011).**

I hold a **PhD in NLP** from the [Tokyo Institute of Technology](https://www.isct.ac.jp/en) (now Institute of Science Tokyo, 2025), where I worked with [Prof. Naoaki Okazaki](https://www.chokkan.org/) on using language models to judge whether sentences are natural and well-formed. I also hold an **M.Tech in Computer Science from [IIT Delhi](https://home.iitd.ac.in/)** and a B.E. in Computer Science from TCET Indore. My work has produced six patent filings and peer-reviewed papers in NLP and deep learning.
